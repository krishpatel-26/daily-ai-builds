# Hiring-focused portfolio builds

These three projects cover customer-facing engineering workflows, revenue operations automation, and robotics telemetry diagnostics.

## 1. Customer Deployment OS — Day 011
Live demo: https://customer-deployment.hatchable.site
Source code: https://github.com/krishpatel-26/daily-ai-builds/tree/main/day-011/01-customer-deployment-os

A local-first workspace for customer signals, evidence capture, launch readiness gates, action planning, and customer handoff briefs. Built as a static browser prototype.

## 2. Autonomous GTM Signal Engine — Day 012
Live demo: https://autonomous-gtm.hatchable.site
Source code: https://github.com/krishpatel-26/daily-ai-builds/tree/main/day-012/01-autonomous-gtm-signal-engine

A prospect qualification workbench with transparent heuristic scoring, duplicate-domain detection, suppression controls, editable outreach drafts, and CSV export.

## 3. Robotics Fleet Diagnostic Copilot — Day 013
Live demo: https://robotics-fleet.hatchable.site
Source code: https://github.com/krishpatel-26/daily-ai-builds/tree/main/day-013/01-robotics-fleet-diagnostic-copilot

A telemetry analysis prototype that imports CSV sensor streams, flags timestamp gaps and potential numeric spikes, charts selected sensor traces, and exports diagnostic reports.

## Maturity and access notes

The hosted apps are currently deployed as personal/private Hatchable projects. To make the permanent demo URLs viewable by recruiters, change each project's visibility in its console settings:
- https://hatchable.com/console/projects/customer-deployment
- https://hatchable.com/console/projects/autonomous-gtm
- https://hatchable.com/console/projects/robotics-fleet

These are early prototypes, not production deployments. Manual test checklists are included; browser QA and full test execution remain outstanding. Readiness and GTM scores are heuristics. Robotics findings are candidate events, not confirmed hardware faults. No customer or business impact is claimed.
