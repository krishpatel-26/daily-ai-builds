# Decision log

## Local-first storage
Chosen to avoid account setup and backend costs. Trade-off: no cross-device sync and limited recovery guarantees.

## Human sends reminders
Chosen to prevent accidental outreach and require payment/recipient verification.

## Transparent heuristic
Chosen instead of an opaque AI score so users can understand why an invoice ranks highly.

## CSV portability
Chosen before integrations to keep the prototype usable alongside existing tools with fewer permissions.
