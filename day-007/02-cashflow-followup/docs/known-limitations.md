# Known limitations

- Browser local storage is device/profile specific and not encrypted.
- No cloud sync, team access, accounting integration, or bank connection.
- No email/SMS/WhatsApp sending or payment verification.
- INR is the display currency; there is no currency field or conversion.
- CSV diagnostics are basic and formula-injection protection remains future work.
- Browser-level end-to-end tests are not included.
- Dates use the browser's local date.
- The priority score is a heuristic, not a trained or validated prediction.
