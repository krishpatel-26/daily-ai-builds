# Dashboard metric definitions

- Outstanding: sum of amounts on records not marked paid.
- Overdue: unpaid records whose due date is before the current local date.
- Due this week: unpaid records due today through seven calendar days ahead.
- Needs follow-up: currently equal to overdue count; it is a queue signal, not proof that contact is appropriate.
- Paid count: records marked paid.

Metrics reflect user-entered data only. They are not verified financial statements or real-time bank balances.
