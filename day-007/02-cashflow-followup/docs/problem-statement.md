# Problem statement

## Observed workflow pain
A freelancer or small business may issue invoices but track them across spreadsheets, sent emails, and calendar reminders. This creates three practical failure modes:

1. **Visibility gap:** the owner cannot quickly see how much is unpaid or overdue.
2. **Prioritization gap:** high-value or long-overdue invoices are mixed with routine records.
3. **Follow-up friction:** writing a polite reminder from scratch creates a reason to postpone it.

## Target user
A solo operator or small team with a modest invoice volume and no need for a full accounting suite.

## Proposed intervention
A single invoice queue with aging/status indicators, a priority sort, and a reminder draft per invoice.

## Evidence status
This is a problem hypothesis to validate with interviews and observation, not a claim based on a completed user study. Interview at least five freelancers or small businesses before treating the prioritization model as validated.
