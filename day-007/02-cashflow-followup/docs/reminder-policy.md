# Reminder policy

Reminder drafts should be polite, factual, and easy to correct. The app:
- identifies the invoice reference and amount,
- mentions whether it is due or overdue,
- asks for an expected payment date or missing documentation,
- leaves the sender identity as a placeholder,
- never sends messages automatically,
- refuses to generate a reminder for records marked paid.

Before sending, the user should confirm that the payment is still outstanding, check the recipient, and follow the terms of their client agreement.
