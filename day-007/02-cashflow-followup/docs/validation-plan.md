# Customer discovery plan

Interview at least five freelancers and two small-business operators.

Ask how they track invoices, decide whom to follow up with first, what makes them postpone reminders, what details they need before sending, and how they back up financial records.

Success signals: users describe a recurring visibility or follow-up problem; a user can add an invoice and draft a reminder in under two minutes; users understand that payment is not verified and messages are not sent automatically.

This small sample is directional, not statistically representative. Record contradictory feedback and revise the hypothesis.
