# Field interview guide

Ask practitioners to walk through a recent customer implementation from observation to resolution.

1. Where did the original evidence live?
2. How was the blocker reproduced?
3. Who owned the next action?
4. What did GTM need to know and what was still uncertain?
5. Where was the handoff delayed or distorted?
6. Which commitments required approval?
7. What data would be inappropriate to store in a shared tool?

Capture workflow steps and contradictions, not just feature requests.
