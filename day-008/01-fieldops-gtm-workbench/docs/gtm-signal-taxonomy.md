# GTM signal taxonomy

- Blocker: a reproducible obstacle to implementation or delivery.
- Adoption: a gap between setup and regular use.
- Opportunity: a customer-stated need that may justify a scoped expansion, subject to validation.
- Product: feedback about usability, capability, or behavior.
- Risk: a delivery, dependency, or renewal concern that needs evidence and an owner.

Taxonomy labels are workflow aids, not definitive account classifications. Record uncertainty explicitly.
