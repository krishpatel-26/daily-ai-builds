# Known limitations

- Local storage is not encrypted and does not sync across devices.
- No CRM, ticketing, email, calendar, or analytics integration.
- Follow-up drafts require human verification and are never sent.
- Priority scores are heuristics and have not been validated against outcomes.
- No authentication or team access controls.
- No browser end-to-end tests included yet.
- Demo signals are fictional and must not be presented as customer results.
- No external AI model is connected; all current logic is deterministic.
