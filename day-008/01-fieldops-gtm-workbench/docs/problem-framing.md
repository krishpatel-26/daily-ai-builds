# Problem framing: the handoff gap

Forward-deployed engineers learn the real constraints while implementing with customers. GTM teams need evidence about adoption, expansion, risk, and customer outcomes. The information often fragments across notes, tickets, and account updates.

## Hypothesis
A shared signal-to-action record can make the evidence, owner, and next step explicit.

## What this prototype does not prove
It does not claim customer interviews have validated the problem or that the tool improves revenue, activation, or retention. Treat examples as synthetic and validate with field teams.
