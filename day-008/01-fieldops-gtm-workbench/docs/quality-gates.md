# Signal quality gates

A high-quality signal should answer:
- What exactly happened?
- Where did the evidence come from?
- What is fact versus interpretation?
- Why does it matter to implementation, adoption, or GTM?
- Who is responsible?
- What is the next verifiable step?
- What must be confirmed before communicating externally?

If evidence is vague, ask a clarifying question instead of increasing priority or inventing a cause.
