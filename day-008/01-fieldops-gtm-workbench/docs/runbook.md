# Field workflow runbook

1. Observe a customer issue or opportunity.
2. Capture the exact evidence and source context without embellishment.
3. Name an owner who can take the next step.
4. Write one concrete, verifiable action.
5. Prioritize transparently and agree on a due date.
6. Validate the proposed fix with the customer.
7. Record the outcome and mark the signal complete only when the agreed outcome is verified.
8. Share a reviewed account brief with GTM; do not turn an unverified observation into a sales claim.
