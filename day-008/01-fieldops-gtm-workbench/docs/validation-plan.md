# Validation plan

Interview 5–8 forward-deployed engineers, solutions engineers, customer success leads, and GTM operators.

Ask:
- Where do implementation blockers get recorded?
- What evidence is needed before escalating a product issue?
- How are expansion signals distinguished from speculation?
- Who owns follow-through after a customer call?
- What makes account handoffs fail?
- Which tools must this integrate with to fit the real workflow?

Success is not a vanity metric: observe whether users can capture evidence, assign an owner, and agree on a next action faster and with fewer missing fields. Record contradictory feedback and revise the workflow.
