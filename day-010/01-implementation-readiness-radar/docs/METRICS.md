# Success metrics to validate

Prototype usability:
- Time required to complete a readiness assessment
- Percentage of checklist items with evidence attached
- Percentage of blockers with a confirmed owner and due date
- Reviewer agreement on priority ordering
- Number of launch surprises discovered after the go/no-go review

Operational outcomes to evaluate later:
- Time from kickoff to first successful end-to-end test
- Number of critical blockers discovered after target date commitment
- Launch delay days attributable to preventable dependencies
- Time from customer acceptance to support handoff

These are proposed measures, not results achieved by this prototype.
