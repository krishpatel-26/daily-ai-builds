# Product requirements

## Objective
Help FDE and GTM teams expose launch dependencies before making a customer commitment.

## Primary user
An implementation owner coordinating technical and customer stakeholders.

## Inputs
Account name, target launch date, and checklist state.

## Outputs
Readiness score, open blocker list, and exportable launch plan.

## Non-goals
Automatic go/no-go decisions, CRM synchronization, external messaging, or evidence verification.

## Guardrails
Display score methodology, highlight critical gates, use synthetic demo data, and require human review.
