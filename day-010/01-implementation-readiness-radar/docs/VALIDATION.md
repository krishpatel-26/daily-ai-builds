# Validation plan

## Manual checks
- Mark and unmark items; verify score and coverage update immediately.
- Confirm a critical security or integration item appears at the top of blockers.
- Set a launch date in the past and future; verify days remaining.
- Export JSON and inspect account, score, completed items, and open items.
- Generate Markdown and verify it contains owners and the launch gate.
- Reset the checklist and verify the plan is hidden.
- Test a narrow mobile viewport and keyboard navigation.

## Risks
- False readiness from unchecked evidence: require evidence links in a future version.
- Misleading score: explain weights and highlight critical gates.
- Unclear accountability: proposed owners must be confirmed by real stakeholders.
- Customer commitment risk: generated plans are drafts and never sent automatically.

## Not yet done
Automated tests, browser QA, accessibility audit, CRM integration, and customer validation.
