# Implementation discovery questions

## Business outcome
- What customer workflow should improve?
- What measurable outcome defines success?
- What is the cost of the current workaround?

## Technical environment
- Which systems and data sources are involved?
- What access, API, or network constraints exist?
- What security review is required?

## Adoption
- Who uses the workflow daily?
- What training and change management are needed?
- How will adoption be measured?

## Launch
- What acceptance tests must pass?
- Who owns go/no-go?
- What monitoring and rollback are required?
- Who receives the support handoff?
