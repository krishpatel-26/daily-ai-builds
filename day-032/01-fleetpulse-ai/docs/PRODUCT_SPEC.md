# Product Specification

## Problem
Maintenance teams need actionable visibility across assets, but raw sensor streams are hard to triage and threshold alerts lack context.

## Product
A fleet health workspace combining operating limits, baseline deviations, trend features, transparent reason codes, and maintenance workflow.

## Personas
Reliability engineer, maintenance planner, operations supervisor, plant data engineer.

## Success measures
Alert precision, false alarms per asset-week, useful warning lead time, work-order closure time, avoided unplanned downtime (measured carefully), and user trust in explanations.
