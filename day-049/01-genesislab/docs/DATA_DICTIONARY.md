# Data dictionary
`temperature`: dimensionless synthetic temperature setting, integer steps of five between 155 and 225. `speed`: dimensionless process speed setting between 20 and 80 in steps of five. `energy_index`: abstract safety proxy, not measured energy. `quality`: simulated process score with deterministic seeded noise. `synthetic_true_quality`: noise-free simulator objective. `uncertainty`: Gaussian process posterior standard deviation in score units. `expected_improvement`: Gaussian expected improvement acquisition value. `cumulative_synthetic_regret`: sum of oracle-minus-selected true objective across experiments.
