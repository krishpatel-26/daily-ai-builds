# Research roadmap

- Stochastic lead-time and transport disruption models.
- Multi-product inventory with perishability and shelf-life.
- Forecast baselines and empirical data splits.
- MILP sourcing/allocation benchmark and constraint feasibility.
- Backorders, partial fill and customer service levels.
- Network graphs and event-sourced inventory histories.
- Uncertainty calibration, sensitivity analysis and cost-validation studies.
