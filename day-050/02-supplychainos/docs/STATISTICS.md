# Statistical methodology

Each policy is evaluated on ten matched seeds, holding exogenous demand streams constant for fair policy comparison. Outputs report mean fill rate, modeled cost, lost sales and paired cost difference against lean. Approximate 95% intervals use mean ± 1.96·sample_standard_deviation/sqrt(n); these are not robust uncertainty guarantees for skewed samples. The Pareto set seeks low modeled cost and high service rate simultaneously.
